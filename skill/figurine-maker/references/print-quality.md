# Quality interpretation

Inspect actual reports and previews, source and output dimensions, units, components, boundary/non-manifold edges and repair changes. A base may be a separate shell and contact is not proof of a printable union. Thin walls, fragile details, support needs, hollow resin drainage, material and printer calibration require separate assessment. Watertightness alone cannot establish print success. Only report slicing or physical testing when performed with identifiable evidence; otherwise UNVERIFIED. Compare before and after views when available and preserve originals.
